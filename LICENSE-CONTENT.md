## What counts as course content

- `lessons/`: objectives, activities, example prompts and passages, answer
  keys, and facilitator notes
- `claims.js`: the text of every claim and its rationale
- the course text in `course.js`: framing, track descriptions and the
  evaluation plan
- `docs/`: lesson designs

Everything else is software and stays under the MIT License in
[LICENSE](LICENSE): the renderer, styles, pages, tests and scripts. The fonts
in `fonts/` keep their own SIL Open Font License; see `fonts/OFL-*.txt`.

## How to credit it

You may share and adapt the content for any purpose, including commercially,
as long as you give credit, link to the licence, and say whether you changed
anything. For example:

> Adapted from *AI Literacy — Using AI Well* by David Petry,
> https://davidpetry-cloud.github.io/ai-literacy-module/, licensed under
> CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: …

A request, not a licence condition: each claim carries its attestation
status. If you adapt a claim, please don't show it as attested unless you
have checked it yourself.
